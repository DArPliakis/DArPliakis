# Demetrios A. Pliakis, PhD
**Mathematical Physicist & Applied Mathematician**  
*ADITAL LLC *

> **Bridging PDE Theory, Geometric Analysis, and Computational Algorithms**  
> Specializing in eigenvalue estimates, free boundary problems, and adaptive solvers for degenerate diffusion equations.  

### Expertise
- **PDEs & Nonlinear Diffusion**: Porous Medium Equation (PME), Reaction-PME (R-PME), Stefan conditions  
- **Geometric Analysis**: Laplacian eigenvalues on hypersurfaces, Hardy inequalities, level sets  
- **Computational Math**: Adaptive mesh refinement, implicit solvers, parallel algorithms  
- **Interdisciplinary**: Real estate modeling (gentrification, market dynamics), quantum/classical physics  

### Featured Work
- **[Hardy-PME-Adaptive](https://github.com/DArPliakis/hardy-pme-adaptive)**: Open-source 2D solver for R-PME with Hardy-guided refinement. 80%+ efficiency gains. [Full Report (PDF)](https://github.com/DArPliakis/hardy-pme-adaptive/blob/main/docs/PME_R_PME.pdf)  
- **Eigenvalue Gaps for Hypersurfaces**: Lower bounds on sphere Laplacians (arXiv:1308.XXXX, 2013)  
- **Publications**: 18+ papers, cited 39x [](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=39TzG-AAAAAJ)
